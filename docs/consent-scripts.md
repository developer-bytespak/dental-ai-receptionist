# Consent and disclosure scripts

Pack version: **v1.0**. Effective date: fill in at go-live.

Every script the agent reads or sends that gives a notice, asks for consent, or acknowledges an opt-out is listed here with an id. The value stored in `consent_events.script_ver` is the pack version (`v1.0`). Any change to any script below creates a new pack version and a row in the change log at the end of this file. Old versions stay in this file so a stored `script_ver` can always be matched to exact wording.

Placeholders in double braces are Retell dynamic variables filled at call time. `{{practice_name}}` and `{{location}}` come from the inbound-call webhook, the rest from the flow.

Rules that apply to all scripts:

- Opening lines are fixed text in the conversation flow, not generated by the model.
- The agent never reads back procedure codes, balances, diagnoses or insurance details.
- Read-backs of appointments contain day, time, provider first name and location only.

## Which script produces which consent_events row

| Script id | consent_events.kind | channel | When the row is written |
|-----------|---------------------|---------|-------------------------|
| CS-01 | recording_notice and ai_disclosure | voice | Every inbound call, by workflow 08 on call_analyzed |
| CS-02 | sms_opt_in | voice | When the caller answers yes, by workflow 08 |
| CS-06 | recording_notice and ai_disclosure | voice | Every outbound reminder call, including voicemail |
| CS-08 | sms_opt_out | sms | On a STOP keyword, by workflow 10 |
| CS-11 | call_opt_out or sms_opt_out | voice | When the caller declines contact on a call, by workflow 08 |

Scripts CS-03, CS-04, CS-05, CS-07, CS-09, CS-10 and CS-12 are disclosure or wording controls and do not create consent rows on their own.

## CS-01 Inbound opening (recording notice and AI disclosure)

Used by: inbound-reception flow, Opening node. Fixed text.

> Thanks for calling {{practice_name}} {{location}}. You're speaking with our automated assistant, and this call is recorded for quality. Say 'staff' at any time to reach a person. How can I help?

Notes: this single line satisfies both the recording notice and the AI disclosure. It must play in full before the agent listens for intent.

## CS-02 SMS reminder opt-in

Used by: inbound-reception flow, after any booking or reschedule, as a separate question.

> Would you like a text reminder to this number before your visit? Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP at any time.

Accepted as yes: "yes", "sure", "please", "okay", "that's fine". Anything else is no. The answer is stored in the `sms_opt_in_given` analysis field. No text is ever sent without a yes.

## CS-03 Queue fallback

Used by: inbound-reception flow, Queue node.

> I've sent that to our team and they'll confirm with you by text or call within the hour.

Notes: used when the patient cannot be identified after two tries, for new patients, when NexHealth returns an error, and in queue-only mode. Outside office hours the wording is "first thing when the office opens" instead of "within the hour".

## CS-04 Transfer during office hours

Used by: inbound-reception flow, Transfer node.

> One moment, I'll connect you with our front desk.

Notes: warm transfer to the location's front-desk line. Triggered by "staff", "person", "human", or any clinical, pain, medication, billing, balance or records topic.

## CS-05 After-hours callback

Used by: inbound-reception flow, Transfer node when the office is closed.

> Our office is closed right now. I can have the team call you back when we open. What's the best number to reach you?

Notes: the number is passed to `queue_request` with reason `after_hours_callback`. Only the last four digits are stored in the queue row.

## CS-06 Outbound reminder opening

Used by: outbound-reminder flow, Opening node. Fixed text.

> Hi, this is an automated call from {{practice_name}} about an appointment on {{day}} at {{time}}. This call is recorded. Press 1 or say yes to confirm, say reschedule to change it, or call us at {{callback}}.

Notes: PLAN.md section 5 gives this line without "This call is recorded." The sentence was added so outbound calls also produce a recording_notice row, which the test pass criteria require.

## CS-07 Voicemail message

Used by: outbound-reminder flow, when voicemail is detected.

> Hi, this is {{practice_name}}. We're calling about an appointment on {{day}} at {{time}}. Please call us at {{callback}}. Thank you.

Notes: practice name, callback number, day and time only. No patient name, no provider, no appointment type.

## CS-08 SMS STOP reply

Used by: workflow 10, sent once after a STOP keyword.

> You're unsubscribed from {{practice_name}} texts.

Keywords matched, case-insensitive, whole message or first word: STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE.

## CS-09 Booking confirmation text

Used by: workflow 08, after a booking or reschedule when `sms_opt_in_given` is true.

> {{practice_name}}: your appointment is {{day}} at {{time}} with {{provider_first_name}}, {{location}}. Reply STOP to opt out.

Notes: no appointment type, no patient name.

## CS-10 Reminder text

Used by: workflow 09, the day before an appointment, only for numbers with an sms_opt_in row and no suppression row.

> {{practice_name}}: reminder of your appointment tomorrow at {{time}}, {{location}}. Call {{callback}} to change it. Reply STOP to opt out.

## CS-11 Verbal opt-out acknowledgement

Used by: inbound-reception flow, when a caller says they do not want texts or calls.

> Understood. We won't send reminders to this number.

Notes: the flow sets the opt-out analysis flag; workflow 08 writes a `suppression_list` row with source `verbal` and a `consent_events` row of kind `sms_opt_out` or `call_opt_out` with channel `voice`.

## CS-12 Insurance answer suffix

Used by: inbound-reception flow, Question node, appended to every insurance answer from the knowledge base.

> We'll verify your coverage before your visit.
