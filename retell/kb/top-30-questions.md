# Top 30 Caller Questions and Approved Answers

Answers the automated assistant may give. Anything about a specific person's treatment, bill, balance, insurance coverage, records, or medications is handed to staff.

1. **What are your hours?**
   Downtown: Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM, Wednesday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Friday 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, closed weekends. Northside: Monday and Wednesday 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM, closed Friday, open the first and third Saturday 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

2. **Are you open on Saturday?**
   The Northside office is open the first and third Saturday of each month from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. The Downtown office is closed on Saturdays.

3. **Are you open on Sunday?**
   No, both offices are closed on Sundays. For a dental emergency, call the main line and follow the after-hours prompts.

4. **Where are you located?**
   Downtown: <STREET_ADDRESS>, Suite <SUITE>, 3rd floor of the <BUILDING_NAME> building. Northside: <STREET_ADDRESS> in the <PLAZA_NAME> shopping center.

5. **Where do I park?**
   Downtown: validated parking in the <GARAGE_NAME> garage (bring your ticket) or metered street parking. Northside: free parking in front of the office.

6. **Do you take my insurance?**
   We are in-network with Delta Dental PPO and Premier, Cigna PPO, Aetna PPO, MetLife, Guardian, United Healthcare, Anthem BCBS, Humana, Principal and Ameritas PPO plans. We do not take HMO or DMO plans. We'll verify your coverage before your visit.

7. **Do you take Medicaid?**
   We accept <STATE> Medicaid dental for children under 21 at the Northside office only. We'll verify your coverage before your visit.

8. **Do you take Medicare?**
   Medicare does not cover routine dental care. We can submit Medicare Advantage dental benefits as an out-of-network provider on request. We'll verify your coverage before your visit.

9. **What if I don't have insurance?**
   We offer an in-house membership plan for $<MEMBERSHIP_PRICE> per year that includes two cleanings, two exams, routine x-rays and 15 percent off other treatment. CareCredit financing is also available.

10. **How much is a cleaning?**
    A routine adult cleaning is generally $110 to $180 before insurance. Your exact cost depends on your plan, and the team will give you an estimate before your visit.

11. **How much is a new patient exam?**
    A new patient exam with x-rays is generally $150 to $250 before insurance.

12. **How much does a crown or a filling cost?**
    General ranges before insurance: fillings $150 to $400, crowns $1,000 to $1,600. A written estimate is given at the office after an exam.

13. **How long is a cleaning appointment?**
    About 45 to 60 minutes for adults and 30 to 45 minutes for children.

14. **Do you see children? From what age?**
    Yes, we see children from age 3 at both offices.

15. **Are you accepting new patients?**
    Yes, both offices are accepting new patients. The team will call or text to set up your first visit.

16. **What do I need to bring to my first visit?**
    A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take. Please arrive 15 minutes early or complete the online forms in advance.

17. **Do you offer emergency appointments?**
    Yes, we hold same-day emergency slots at each office. If you are in pain or have swelling or an injury, say "staff" and you will be connected right away.

18. **What is your cancellation policy?**
    Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations or no-shows may be charged $50, waived the first time and for emergencies.

19. **Can I reschedule my appointment by phone?**
    Yes. The assistant can move your appointment after confirming your full name and date of birth.

20. **Can I confirm my appointment by phone?**
    Yes. The assistant can confirm it after checking your full name and date of birth.

21. **How do I stop the text reminders?**
    Reply STOP to any of our texts, or tell the assistant or the team that you don't want texts.

22. **Do you offer teeth whitening?**
    Yes. In-office whitening is $400 to $650 and take-home trays are $250 to $400, before any promotions. Whitening is scheduled by the team after an exam.

23. **Do you do Invisalign or braces?**
    We offer Invisalign consultations at the Downtown office. We do not place traditional braces and refer to an orthodontist for that.

24. **Do you offer sedation?**
    Nitrous oxide is available for most procedures at both offices. Oral sedation is available at the Downtown office. Whether sedation is appropriate for you is a question for the dentist.

25. **Do you do root canals and extractions?**
    Yes, both are done in office. Impacted wisdom teeth and complex cases are referred to an oral surgeon.

26. **Do you do implants?**
    Yes, implant consultations are available at the Downtown office. Placement is generally $1,800 to $3,000 per implant before the crown.

27. **Which dentist will I see?**
    Downtown: Dr. <LastName1> and Dr. <LastName2>. Northside: Dr. <LastName3>, and Dr. <LastName1> on Tuesday evenings. You can ask for a specific dentist when booking.

28. **Is the office wheelchair accessible?**
    Yes, both offices have step-free entrances and accessible treatment rooms and restrooms.

29. **How do I get a copy of my records or x-rays?**
    Records requests are handled by the front desk with a signed release form. Say "staff" and the team will help.

30. **Can I talk to a person?**
    Yes. Say "staff" at any time and you will be connected to the front desk during office hours. After hours, the assistant will take a callback number and the team will call you back.
