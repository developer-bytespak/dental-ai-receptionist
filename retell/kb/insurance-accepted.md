# Insurance and Payment

## Dental insurance plans we are in-network with
Both offices accept the same plans.

- Delta Dental PPO and Delta Dental Premier
- Cigna Dental PPO
- Aetna Dental PPO
- MetLife PDP Plus
- Guardian DentalGuard Preferred
- United Healthcare Dental PPO
- Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield Dental PPO
- Humana Dental PPO
- Principal Dental PPO
- Ameritas PPO
- <STATE> Medicaid dental (children under 21 only, Northside office only)

## Plans we do NOT participate in
- Dental HMO / DMO plans of any carrier (for example Delta Dental DeltaCare, Cigna DHMO, Aetna DMO)
- Discount dental cards
- Medicare Advantage dental benefits (we can submit as out-of-network on request)

If your plan is not listed, we can usually still see you as an out-of-network provider and file the claim for you. Coverage is verified before every visit, so please call with your insurance details or bring your card.

## What the assistant can and cannot say about insurance
- The assistant can say whether a carrier is on the in-network list above.
- The assistant cannot look up an individual's coverage, deductible, remaining benefit, or claim status. The team handles those questions.
- The assistant never asks for an insurance member id over the phone.

## Payment options
- Cash, personal check, all major credit and debit cards, HSA and FSA cards.
- CareCredit financing (6 and 12 month promotional plans, subject to approval).
- In-house membership plan for patients without insurance: $<MEMBERSHIP_PRICE> per year, includes two cleanings, two exams, routine x-rays, and 15 percent off other treatment.

## Payment policy
- Estimated patient portion is due at the time of service.
- We file insurance claims on your behalf for in-network and most out-of-network plans.
- Billing and balance questions are handled by the front desk team, not the automated assistant.
