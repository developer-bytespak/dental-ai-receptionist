# Office Policies

## Cancellation and rescheduling
- Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment.
- Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed without notice, may be charged a $50 late-cancellation fee. The fee is waived for the first occurrence and for documented emergencies.
- Appointments of 90 minutes or longer require 48 hours' notice.
- Three missed appointments in 12 months may result in the office asking for a deposit before booking further visits.
- You can cancel or reschedule by phone at any time, including through the automated assistant.

## Late arrival
- If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, we may need to shorten or reschedule the visit so other patients are not delayed.

## New patients
- Please arrive 15 minutes early or complete the online forms sent by text or email before the visit.
- Bring a photo ID and your insurance card if you have one.
- New patient visits include an exam, necessary x-rays, and a cleaning when time allows.

## Children
- Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian for the first visit and for any treatment requiring consent.
- A parent or guardian may book, reschedule, cancel or confirm a child's appointment by phone after identifying the child by full name and date of birth.

## Appointment reminders
- We send reminders by text and automated phone call before appointments.
- Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts. Tell the assistant or the team "don't text me" and we will note it.
- Message and data rates may apply.

## Recording and the automated assistant
- Calls are recorded for quality and training of our staff.
- Our automated phone assistant can book, reschedule, cancel and confirm appointments and answer general office questions. It does not give medical advice, discuss treatment or billing, or access dental records. Say "staff" at any time to reach a person.

## Privacy
- We follow HIPAA. Our Notice of Privacy Practices is available at the front desk and on our website at <WEBSITE>/privacy.
- The automated assistant only asks for your full name and date of birth to locate your record. It will never ask for your social security number, insurance member id, address, or email.
- Requests for dental records or x-rays are handled by the front desk with a signed release form. Allow up to 30 days.

## Payment
- Estimated patient portion is due at the time of service.
- Balance and billing questions go to the front desk team, not the automated assistant.

## Prescriptions and refills
- Prescription and refill requests are handled by the clinical team during office hours. The automated assistant cannot take these requests; ask for staff.

## Referrals
- We refer to trusted oral surgeons, endodontists, periodontists and orthodontists when needed. The front desk sends the referral and your x-rays with your consent.

## Service animals and accommodations
- Service animals are welcome. Please tell the front desk about any accommodation you need.
