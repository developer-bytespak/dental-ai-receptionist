## Appointment types the assistant can book by phone
- Cleaning (includes a periodic exam)
- Exam (new patient exam or a limited exam)
- Emergency visit (same-day or next-day slot; the team calls back with details)
- Other (the team calls back to schedule)

Crowns, root canals, extractions, implants, whitening and orthodontic consultations are scheduled by the front desk team after an exam.

## General questions about procedures
**How often should I have a cleaning?** Most patients are seen every six months; some are seen every three or four months on a dentist's recommendation.

**Do you see children?** Yes, from age 3 at both offices.

**Do you offer sedation?** Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) is available for most procedures at both offices. Oral sedation is available with an appointment with Dr. <LastName1> at the Downtown office. Questions about whether sedation is right for an individual go to the team.

**Do you do Invisalign or braces?** We offer Invisalign consultations at the Downtown office. We do not provide traditional braces and will refer to an orthodontist.

**How long does a filling take?** Usually 30 to 60 minutes depending on the size and number of fillings.

**Will I be numb after a visit?** For fillings and most other treatment, local anesthetic typically lasts one to three hours. The assistant cannot give individual medical guidance; the team or the dentist will explain what to expect at the visit.

**Do you provide emergency care?** Yes, we reserve same-day emergency slots at each office. If you are in severe pain, have swelling, or have a knocked-out tooth, ask for staff and you will be connected right away.
